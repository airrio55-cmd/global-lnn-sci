# Methodology

Primary differential: d_t = loss_A,t - loss_B,t. Negative favors A; positive favors B. For focal comparisons A is the learned model, B the frozen benchmark. No sign reversal is permitted. Brier uses equal-weight arithmetic mean saved five-seed probabilities for learned models and0/1 probabilities for rules. PT, BA and MacroF1 use binary3-of-5 majority, with deterministic consensus verified for logistic andLightGBM. Neither labels nor probability averaging are tuned. Source predictions, selected losses and thresholds remain unchanged.

DM: sample mean differential divided by sqrt(Bartlett Newey-West long-run variance/N), lag19, biased autocovariances denominatorN. HLN factor sqrt((N+1-2H+H(H-1)/N)/N),H20, applied uniformly; two-sided Student t reference dfN-1. Zero/invalid variance yields undefined status, never a manufactured p-value. All78 Brier pairs and28 secondary learned-only logloss pairs per market are stored. Logloss eps1e-12. Three benchmark-specific40-test focal Holm families are separate; PE sensitivity is a separate5-test family. Raw full pairwise results are not an invitation to unadjusted favorable pair selection.

GW-style conditional moment Wald: g_t=z_t*d_t, z=(1,most_recent_resolved_d,previous_raw_abs_LogRet,previous_raw_Vol20). A loss for target j resolves at j+20; it is usable only when that date is no later than target t-1. Thus naive d[t-1] is forbidden. Missing unresolved early rows are excluded uniformly, and an availability assertion is applied. Instruments are rescaled by fixed1,1,.01,.01 for numerical conditioning; this invertible transformation leaves Wald unchanged. Statistic N*gbar' S_HAC^-1*gbar,chi-square4df,lag19. Singular covariance returns undefined. This is not claimed as exact original GW: original rolling forecast-estimation assumptions are not met by saved expanding-fit forecasts. A significant result tests equality of conditional moments, not one model's superiority; consult mean loss differential for direction.

PT: unchanged previous validated Pesaran–Timmermann implementation, conventional two-sided p-values. Positive support requires positive statistic and adjusted p<.05. Significant negative association never counts as positive skill. Undefined constant-forecast PT retains originalNaN, while an input1 placeholder conservatively preserves all planned Holm family sizes; adjusted result remainsNaN and supportFalse. A30 neural, B40 learned, C5 LTC, D5 paired PE DM are fixed in protocol_lock.json. Family B is the broad primary classification in the conclusion matrix; family A is additionally reported explicitly for neural models. No prior Holm output is overwritten.

MCS: arch8.0.0 MCS rangeR statistic, stationary joint-row bootstrap, expected block length20,10000 replicates,seed20261106. All eleven predefined candidates are retained even if thresholded learned majority is constant; only prespecified Always-Up/Down are outside MCS. One elimination path supplies monotone MCS p-values and both90%/95% membership. P>.10 means retained at90%;p>.05 at95%. Elimination order is recorded, including the final survivor with p1. Retention does not mean equivalent expected losses.

SPA: Hansen studentized maximum of positive standardized mean improvements, Persistence minus alternative loss. Joint stationary-bootstrap means supply long-run variances N*var(bootstrap_mean). Consistent recentering keeps means not below -sigma/sqrtN*sqrt(2loglogN), otherwise zero. Observed and bootstrap maxima include zero. P=(1+count(bootstrap_stat>=observed))/(10001). This corrects for the fixed ten-alternative search within each market; no additional across-market SPA Holm family was requested. All ten alternatives remain. Implementation is independently compared against arch8 SPA on identically standardized data and common draws. The arch8 studentize flag by itself did not scale simulated differentials in the inspected source, so the explicit verified studentized implementation is used and not silently mislabeled. Source hashes are archived.

Reduced overlap: fixed phase0,step20 saved prediction rows. Effective forecast stepH1 gives DM HAC0/HLNH1, prespecified; full data always HAC19/H20. Approximately47-49 observations imply low power. PT and focal reduced DM are raw diagnostics, not newly corrected positive-skill claims. MCS not run below100 per fixed rule. Reduced-overlap evaluation lowers mechanical label overlap but does not establish statistical independence and substantially reduces sample size.

Economic columns copy the existing10bps long/cash results without new assumptions or economic tests. Extreme exposure<.1/>.9 and forecast-up<.1/>.9 are flags, never exclusions. Conclusion categories follow the frozen rule, are descriptive and never label a model robust based on correlated diagnostics. Focal DM wins require mean d<0 and family Holm<.05; significant d>0 favors benchmark. The same market days underlie probability and classification metrics but those forecast objects differ; Brier can favor softened forecasts over deterministic extremes even when BA is weak.
