# Methodology and limitations

## Validation-only architecture selections
All15 losses are selected within architecture using only Model/Loss/WF_Val_BA_mean from each existing summary.csv. Expected selections are verified before test rows are opened. SP500 uses sp500_rerun exclusively. This is a retrospective manuscript reanalysis, not a newly untouched test set: prior inspection/design uncertainty is not erased. No predictive fit, checkpoint modification, threshold search, feature change, HMM fit or regime update occurs.

75 seed rows include all five seeds of each selected architecture-market pair, no seed selection. Each saved Probability>original Threshold yields its binary prediction. Every metric reproduces the existing seed result within absolute1e-12; full dates, labels and benchmark definition are audited. Architecture-level means/SD are separate from majority-vote metrics.

## Seed and architecture inference
Wilcoxon tests are exact two-sided conditional enumeration of all2^n nonzero signed-rank sign assignments. Zeros are discarded; absolute ties use midranks. All-zero differences yield p1. This avoids reporting ordinary tie-free exact distributions for tied ranks. Seeds still share a test history and are not independent market samples. Primary Holm family=15 architecture-market BA comparisons. Additional LNN-only Holm family=5; both are labeled, and manuscript table gives Holm15 primary plus Holm5 supplemental. Ten-thousand bootstrap resamples of five seeds with replacement, seed20261028, quantify algorithmic seed variability only, not independent market-history uncertainty. n5 limits power; smallest possible two-sided exact p with five nonzero differences is0.0625. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence.

Friedman compares validation-selected LNN/LSTM/Transformer means over five market blocks; BA is primary and Macro F1 secondary. Report chi-square asymptotic p, Kendall W=chi-square/[5*(3−1)], and Holm over the two omnibus tests. Five markets provide low power and are economically correlated. The analysis did not detect a stable cross-market architecture ranking, although the small number of market blocks limits inferential power. Do not infer architectural equivalence or a universal winner.

## Paired majority and reduced overlap
All five separately thresholded seed votes give an Up majority at>=3; probabilities are not averaged. Full-test LNN majority predictions are checked against the validation-selected economic operational predictions, never the old test-selected Best configuration. One McNemar per market compares majority vs Persistence; primary p=max(two-sided exact binomial, continuity-corrected chi-square), Holm5. n00=both wrong,n01=Persistence correct/LNN wrong,n10=LNN correct/Persistence wrong,n11=both correct. McNemar tests correctness/Accuracy, NOT BA. H20 labels overlap and ordinary pair-independence assumptions are imperfect, so full-test p-values are supportive, not definitive.

Reduced-overlap uses original sorted prediction rows0,20,40,... only. No alternate offset is evaluated. Actual frozen-market observation spacing is verified>=20. Labels/votes/Persistence are exact saved rows, selected dates explicitly stored. Full/subset comparisons are majority-vote contrasts. Reduced-overlap lowers mechanical label overlap but does not establish full independence; small N lowers power. Full and reduced-overlap McNemar have separate clearly labeled Holm5 families, not a pooled or seed-level25-test family. Existing primary/full statistical and old nonoverlap folders are preserved.

## Nikkei High estimand
Only NIKKEI225 LNN MILD_WCE is used, not CE. Primary LNN absolute metrics are arithmetic means of the five seed metrics; N117 means117 paired dates, not585 independent seed-date observations. Persistence appears once per date. The expected BA0.5392/Persistence0.2923/delta0.2468 are verified within0.0005 rounding tolerance. Majority metrics are separately labeled as sensitivity. Full absolute accuracy, BA, Macro F1, Recall, Precision, F1 and predicted positive rate are reported. BA slightly above0.5 and a very weak Persistence baseline require caution despite a large relative delta.

Although117 daily High-regime observations are available, the20-day forecast horizon induces substantial dependence among adjacent labels, so the effective amount of independent information is materially smaller than the raw observation count. No numerical effective sample size is estimated. Constructed episode/block counts below describe the randomization design only, not independent observations.

## Nikkei moving-block bootstrap
Noncircular blocks of20 are drawn from the FULL chronological saved test date sequence, concatenated/truncated to original full length. Only after sampling are fixed High labels used to subset. This preserves chronology within blocks and avoids treating disconnected High episodes as contiguous for bootstrap construction. Y_true, all five seed predictions, deterministic Persistence and regime labels share sampled indices. Statistic is mean five-seed BA minus baseline BA; Macro F1 and Accuracy use the same paired mean metric contrast. Use10000 iterations, seed20261028. Samples with no High rows or only one High target class have undefined BA and are excluded transparently, never replaced. The same valid subset is used for the three metric intervals; valid/invalid counts and raw samples are saved.

Percentile95% intervals are conditional on the already estimated HMM sequence and do not incorporate HMM refitting, regime-model uncertainty, model-selection uncertainty or multiplicity across regime cells. Resampling blocks approximates dependence rather than guaranteeing full independence. A nominally positive interval is not a multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory claim.

## Episode-restricted exploratory block permutation
Identify consecutive High observations in the original frozen target observation calendar. A missing evaluation observation also splits an episode; no block crosses a non-High interruption or an unobserved gap. Within each episode create chunks of maximum20, keeping shorter end chunks. Thus block lengths can be shorter than20; this is reported, not hidden. Each whole-block coin flip swaps each seed's LNN predictions with a paired copy of Persistence, jointly across all five seeds. Y_true is fixed. The statistic remains the arithmetic mean of five seed BA contrasts, matching the descriptive primary contrast; it is not a single favorable seed or a majority-based replacement statistic.

Because BA difference is linear in class-weighted correctness at fixed Y_true, implement block-swap randomization by sign flipping block contributions. Literal full prediction swaps are independently checked for initial permutation patterns.10000 permutations, seed20261028, two-sided plus-one Monte Carlo p=(1+exceedances)/(B+1), using abs(permuted delta)>=abs(observed delta). Shared swaps preserve the fact that the seeds share dates and baseline. Raw permutation samples and episode/block boundaries are saved.

The null assumes exchangeability under model/baseline swaps at block level. This assumption is not guaranteed for dependent financial labels; forecasts can remain dependent across episode/block boundaries. Seven constructed episodes/nine blocks, if observed, are not an estimated effective sample size. Short episodes may limit dependence protection. The test is explicitly exploratory, post-hoc, not preregistered and not confirmatory. It tests a different conditional randomization construction from the moving-block bootstrap, so weak permutation evidence and a nominally positive bootstrap interval are both reported, not reconciled by selecting a favorable method.

## Interpretation policy
Before sensitivity computation, the script records a conservative rule: Outcome A requires absolute High BA>0.5, positive BA CI lower bound, nonnegative Nikkei reduced-overlap delta and exploratory permutation p<=0.05; otherwise Outcome B. This is an interpretation rule, not a signal/model selection rule. Neither outcome allows retuning. A weak exploratory p does not demonstrate no effect, but warrants narrowing claims. If Outcome B occurs, the manuscript should emphasize benchmark/negative results and statistically fragile descriptive regime differences rather than strong conditional-generalization claims. Existing economic results are not changed.
